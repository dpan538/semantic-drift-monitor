## Working Title

Semantic Gap, Scenario Lock-In, and Trust Response in Sunscreen Consumer Reviews: A Feasibility Study Using Amazon Reviews 2023

## Abstract

Consumer reviews contain more than evaluations of satisfaction; they also record how consumers interpret product claims, usage situations, and experiential failures. This study develops a reproducible marketing analytics pipeline for measuring semantic mismatch, trust response, and scenario lock-in in sunscreen and SPF skincare reviews. Using Amazon Reviews 2023, the analysis constructs a feasibility sample of 100 SPF products, 50,000 reviews, and 9,124 product-month observations from 2005 to 2023. Product metadata are treated as static semantic descriptors and expanded to product-month observations only for feasibility modelling. Fixed-effects panel models show that lagged semantic gap is not a significant predictor of a response-only trust index, but is significantly associated with lower scenario diversity and higher scenario lock-in. Keyword-level analysis shows strong negative drift for “white cast,” while “natural” and “mineral” do not show comparable devaluation. High-SPF products show higher skepticism and more white-cast, greasy-texture, and burn-failure complaints. The findings suggest that semantic mismatch may first appear as use-case narrowing rather than generalized distrust.
