- 50,000 reviews;
- 9,124 product-month rows;
- 2005-03-18 to 2023-05-12 review span;
- response-only trust index does not show significant lagged semantic-gap effect;
- lock-in index shows significant positive lagged semantic-gap effect;
- white cast shows clear negative keyword drift;
- natural/mineral do not show the expected negative drift in this category.
